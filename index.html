What is P-C-A?

Ganit's Unique Appraisal Philosophy

Ganit follows a differentiated performance appraisal model called P-C-A. Unlike standard rating systems, P-C-A evaluates you across three dimensions that reflect both your individual output and your impact on the organisation.

The Annual Cycle

The performance cycle runs from January to December each calendar year. Key events:

  • Goals are set at the start of the year through discussion with your manager
  • A quarterly review process is followed for goal-setting, reviews, and scores
  • Increment and variable pay are paid during Q1 of the following year
  • Promotions are determined by performance, special projects, and role availability
Eligibility Cut-off
Joined on or before 30 September of the calendar year

Goal Setting & KPIs

🔥 Passion – KPIs for Customer Delight

Your manager will discuss and agree on measurable KPIs that reflect how you contribute to customer satisfaction.

  • Number of appreciation emails or mentions received from the customer
  • Extension of projects — a sign the customer values your work
  • Quality and timeliness of deliverables that directly impact the customer

🏗️ Contribution – Building Ganit's Brand

This pillar measures how actively you build and represent Ganit beyond your daily project work.

  • Number of articles published on Ganit's LinkedIn, Blog, or Website
  • Participating in conferences or industry events
  • Introducing new prospects or business leads to Ganit
  • Number of hiring referrals made

🌱 Attitude – Self & Team Development

Attitude measures personal growth, team enablement, and your commitment to Ganit's culture.

  • Number of new certifications obtained
  • Number of learning sessions conducted for your team
  • Efforts put in to build Ganit internally
  • Commitment and aspiration — being a role model to others
  • Personal drive to develop leadership and technical skills
  • Domain expertise — e.g., peers should say "She/he is the Python go-to person"
💡

Important: Goals can be updated every quarter based on project requirements and discussions with your manager. You own your PCA — no one else. Ensure timely completion.

Roles, Promotions & Responsibilities

Employee Responsibilities

You own the entire APR (Annual Performance Review) process. This includes:

  • Setting your goals in consultation with your manager at the start of every quarter
  • Completing self-assessments every quarter — on time
  • Ensuring quarterly reviews are conducted and not missed
  • Engaging in meaningful discussions with your manager about performance
  • Updating goals every quarter if project or role requirements change

Manager Responsibilities

  • Setting clear, measurable, and agreed-upon goals with each team member
  • Providing regular, constructive feedback throughout the year
  • Conducting quarterly evaluations promptly and fairly
  • Supporting employee development and growth aspirations

What It Takes to Get Promoted

Promotions at Ganit are merit-based and multi-dimensional. Tenure alone does not guarantee promotion. The key criteria:

Performance
Strong ratings in your current role over a sustained period
Tenure
Minimum 1 year in current role required
Stretch Roles
Successfully taking on responsibilities beyond your current level
Role Availability
Open positions must exist; promotions also depend on company needs
🌟

Also evaluated: cross-skilling, cultural alignment, demonstrating next-level competencies, and involvement in special projects.
